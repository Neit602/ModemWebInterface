          Search for all networks?
          
    
          The modem has to leave the network to search. The internet on this router drops for 1–3 minutes, then the modem reconnects by itself.
          
    
          
      
      
    
          
  
          
          


  
          
    
          Scan for Wi-Fi networks nearby?
          
    
          The router listens on every channel, about 6 seconds per band. While it does, Wi-Fi on this router blips: devices may pause for a moment. Nothing is changed.
